## Objective
To build a better understanding of Probability and Statistics.


## Syllabus and Unit Index

The Statistics component is organised into the following twelve units. Chapter folders are study groupings based on the syllabus; they are not official chapter divisions.

| Unit | Subject | Main areas |
|:---:|---|---|
| 01 | [Calculus](units/01_calculus/) | Sets, sequences, series, limits, differentiation, integration, multivariable calculus |
| 02 | [Matrix Theory](units/02_matrix_theory/) | Vector spaces, linear systems, inner products, eigenvalues, quadratic forms, SVD |
| 03 | [Probability](units/03_probability/) | Probability axioms, conditioning, random variables, moments, transformations, inequalities |
| 04 | [Standard Univariate Distributions](units/04_standard_univariate_distributions/) | Discrete and continuous distributions, their properties and applications |
| 05 | [Joint Distributions](units/05_joint_distributions/) | Joint and conditional laws, random vectors, order statistics, sampling distributions |
| 06 | [Convergence of Random Variables](units/06_convergence_of_random_variables/) | Modes of convergence, limit lemmas, laws of large numbers, central limit theorem |
| 07 | [Stochastic Processes](units/07_stochastic_processes/) | Markov chains, Poisson processes, birth-and-death processes, Brownian motion |
| 08 | [Estimation](units/08_estimation/) | Sufficiency, completeness, unbiased estimation, estimation methods, confidence intervals |
| 09 | [Testing of Hypotheses](units/09_testing_of_hypotheses/) | Most powerful tests, MLR, UMP and UMPU tests, likelihood ratio tests, large-sample tests |
| 10 | [Non-parametric Statistics](units/10_non_parametric_statistics/) | Empirical distributions, goodness-of-fit tests, rank tests, rank correlations |
| 11 | [Multivariate Analysis](units/11_multivariate_analysis/) | Multivariate normal and Wishart distributions, Hotelling’s test, correlation |
| 12 | [Regression Analysis](units/12_regression_analysis/) | Linear regression, model fit, quadratic forms, estimation and inference |

Unit numbering follows the syllabus. It does not prescribe the order of study.

[General Aptitude](general_aptitude/) is maintained separately; its chapter structure is pending.
